Coordinates:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Creates a figure with x,y,z coordinates at the origin.
   The figure has a black background and white axes.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   h = Coordinates( m )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   m             (1)     Magnitude of each axis
   

   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   h             (1)     Figure handle

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
