Nu2M:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Converts true anomaly to mean anomaly.

   Since version 1.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   [meanAnom,eccAnom] = Nu2M( e, nu )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   e             (1,1)  Eccentricity
   nu            (1,:)  True anomaly

   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   meanAnom       (1,:)  Mean anomaly
   eccAnom        (1,:)  Eccentric anomaly

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
