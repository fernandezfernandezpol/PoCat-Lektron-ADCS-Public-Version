M2E:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Generate the eccentric anomaly from the mean anomaly and the
   eccentricity.

   Since version 1.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   eccAnom  = M2E( e, meanAnom, tol, nMax )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   e            (: or 1) Eccentricity
   meanAnom     (:)      Mean anomaly
   tol          (1,1)    Tolerance
   nMax         (1,1)    Maximum number of iterations

   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   eccAnom     (:)       Eccentric anomaly

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
