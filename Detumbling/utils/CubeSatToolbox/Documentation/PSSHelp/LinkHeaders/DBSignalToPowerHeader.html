DBSignalToPower:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Convert decibels to power.

   Type DBSignalToPower for a demo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Form:
   power = DBSignalToPower( dB )
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

   ------
   Inputs
   ------
   powerDB       (1,:)  Power (dB)
                        
   -------
   Outputs
   -------
   power         (1,:)  Power (W)
                        
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Common: Graphics/Plot2D
